Health Compass

An internal initiative of Alberta Health Shared Services, built on Snowflake, helping approved users turn governed data into better decisions for Albertans. Outputs are AI-generated and require human review before use in decisions or reporting, and Health Compass is not a clinical system. Use is governed by the Health Compass Usage Charter.

How It Works

From sign-in to insight in a few steps

1

Step 1

Sign in using Single Sign-On

Launch the Health Compass and, when prompted, click the Sign in using Single Sign-On button. Username and password sign-in will not work.

2

Step 2

Ask your question

Ask any question about Alberta's health system in plain English. No technical knowledge required.

"Compare median ED wait times between Edmonton and Calgary by CTAS level."

3

Step 3

Explore & verify

Ask follow-ups, filter, and export. Look for the verified query badge for metrics that match approved definitions. Always apply human review before acting on results.

Outputs require human review before use

Data Sources

Integrated, governed, and standardized

Patient Activity

Admissions, discharges, transfers, and emergency department visits.

System Capacity

Bed occupancy, theatre utilization, staffing ratios, and wait times.

Clinical Costing

Cost per weighted activity, resource use, and procedure-level costing.

Quality & Outcomes

Readmission rates, length of stay, and complication rates.

Workforce

FTE counts, agency usage, and sick leave rates.

Clinical Support Services

Volumes, turnaround times, and utilization.
